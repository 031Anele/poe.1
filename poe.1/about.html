Our Story

Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena was founded in 2022 to provide a fun and engaging gaming and esports environment where customers can play games, take part in tournaments, and enjoy gaming-related activities.

We chose this scenario because gaming and esports are very popular among young people, and our mission is to make gaming accessible and professional in Durban.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission

To provide high-performance gaming facilities and host competitive esports events.

Vision

To become KwaZulu-Natal's leading esports hub.
